# Content Plan: 6-Month Editorial Roadmap (ruhanir.com/news)

> **Status:** All entries are strictly **proposals** for the organization's editorial team. None are published. No fabricated statistics, testimonials, or simulated client stories are included.

---

## 1. Editorial Principles
1. **Truthfulness First:** Write only from real operational experience, actual software architecture implemented, and genuine products stocked.
2. **Support Divisions:** Root articles should direct qualified institutional interest to `it.ruhanir.com` or consumer inquiries to `store.ruhanir.com`.
3. **Bilingual Quality:** Bengali articles must be composed naturally in Bangla, not mechanical translations.

```markdown

title: "Descriptive, Factual Headline under 60 Characters"
date: "YYYY-MM-DD"
summary: "Truthful summary of the post (120-150 characters) without hype."
slug: "clean-hyphenated-english-slug"
lang: "en" # or "bn"
author: "Ruhanir Engine"
image: "/images/news/sample-real-photo.webp"
imageAlt: "Accurate description of what is depicted in the photograph"
division: "Ruhanir IT" # or "Ruhanir Store" / "Ruhanir Engine"
```
