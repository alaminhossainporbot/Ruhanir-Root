## 2. THE ROOT SITE AS DESIGNED
- Stack (verify in code): Next.js (App Router) with TypeScript and Tailwind, static export, self-hosted fonts (Inter for English, Noto Sans Bengali for Bangla), no database, no tracking. Bilingual routes /en and /bn with a crawlable language chooser at "/", hreflang with x-default, language switch as normal links, copy in structured content files per language (src/content/en.ts and bn.ts or equivalent), Bangla fallback for the redirect.
- Pages: Home (hero with the large logo mark in a 7/5 split, an "at a glance" strip with divisions, coverage and registration, a divisions section with ordinal panels, an About summary with four principles as a ruled list, a navy contact band, light footer with four columns), About, Businesses, News (Markdown posts per language, RSS, one launch post), Contact (no map, no address; form with honeypot, minimum-time check, Turnstile, an endpoint set by environment variable or a mailto fallback, never a fake success message), Legal hub and policy pages in English and Bangla with one effective date from a single config value, a bilingual 404, sitemap, robots, favicons, Open Graph images.
- Design rules: brand tokens only (teal #42D1CC decorative only, never text; cyan #1499C8; royal #0E52C3 for links and buttons; navy #05308A for primary buttons and the contact band; heading #0B1B3A; body #1F2937; secondary #4B5563; band #F5F7FA; border #E2E8F0); gradients only inside the logo; no emojis; no marketing filler or trust adjectives; no scroll-reveal animations; 1200px container, 8px spacing scale, 96px section padding on desktop and 64px on mobile; body 17 to 18px, nothing below 14px; Bangla line-height 1.8 and headings 1.35 to 1.45; accessible (AA contrast, visible focus, keyboard); mobile-first; Bangla pages use Bangla digits for years, dates and the license number, phone numbers stay in Latin digits.
- Content rules: no invented facts, statistics, awards, clients, testimonials, team members or addresses; every claim must be supportable; missing facts are listed for the owner instead of filled with generic text.

## 3. THE BRAND KIT (this project is its source of truth)
Folder brand-kit/ contains: tokens (Tailwind theme, colors, type scale, spacing, radii), fonts (setup and files), assets (logo files, optimized WebP sizes, favicons, an OG image template), components (Logo, Header, Footer, LanguageSwitch, Button, Section or Container, Prose for policy pages; header and footer are data-driven from a config), company.ts (single source for the organization's names in English and Bangla, emails, phone, trade license line in both digit systems, and the URLs of the three sites), legal-content/ (all policy texts as Markdown in English and Bangla with frontmatter), a README, and later brand-kit/seo/ (metadata builder, hreflang and canonical helpers, JSON-LD builders, sitemap and robots generators, OG image generator, seo-audit script). The kit has been copied by hand into the other two projects: Ruhanir-Store (Next.js, uses it directly) and Ruhanir-IT (TanStack Start with Tailwind v4: components were ported, tokens translated to CSS-first). The logo is a segmented "R" in a teal to navy palette. Only raster files exist (PNG and WebP); a hand-made SVG vectorization was tried but is NOT clean enough to ship; do not use it for brand-critical uses and do not claim a vector logo exists. The Bangla spelling and the license line come from company.ts everywhere.
